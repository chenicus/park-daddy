# Pacific Street and Beach Avenue Street View follow-up

Checked 2026-09-22 using Google Street View. This is a block-level visual pass, not a continuous curb survey. Imagery is mostly August 2024, so it cannot establish current regulations. No additional public free curb section was clear enough to add beyond the short signed sections already in `data/beach-pacific-street-view.json` and the existing city-PDF sections. Parked vehicles alone were not treated as evidence of free parking.
