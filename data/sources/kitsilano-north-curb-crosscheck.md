Reviewed: 2026-09-22

Source map: https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/residential-permit-parking-kitsilano-north.pdf

The City map shows separate curb bars and lists full-time permit, timed public parking with permit exceptions, and no-parking variants. Its purple fill describes resident permit eligibility, not curb parking. The map instructs drivers to refer to posted signs.
