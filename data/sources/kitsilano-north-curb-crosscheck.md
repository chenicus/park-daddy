## Legacy review markers replaced by mapped curb faces

The enforcement-derived points `2100 W 2Nd Av` and `2000 W 2Nd Av` are side-less block estimates. At each point, the Kitsilano North PDF has separate curb bars on both sides of W 2nd. The north face is two-hour public parking, 9am–6pm Mon–Fri, except with permit; the south face is full-time permit parking. These two old `Check signs` points are excluded from the map in favor of those existing side-specific lines. This does **not** classify the whole block or sharpen the schematic bar endpoints.

An August 2011 [Street View panorama near 2145 W 2nd](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=wq3VoJ1Ub_N0yrZNQH-RXg&heading=270&pitch=0&fov=45) shows parking-sign poles along W 2nd but no legible sign face. It is too old to verify the current curb rule. The two PDF classifications remain marked `pdf-guide`; the other four legacy points near the north map are unresolved because the map does not cover both curb faces at their point or they lie outside its bar coverage.
