## Scope and conclusion

This first pass inspected imagery at all 14 currently unsuppressed older inferred-free markers in the downtown/West End area selected from the data (49.274–49.300 latitude, −123.150 to −123.100 longitude, excluding Creelman on the south shore). The 1100 Burnaby and 1300 Broughton estimates are already suppressed by the newer PDF guides and were not rechecked here. PDF-derived curbs and ordinary meter records were not part of this pass.

None of these 14 whole-block free claims was verified. This does **not** prove that every curb on those blocks is paid or prohibited. It means these broad markers lack enough evidence to direct a driver to a specific free space. The strongest readable conflicts are the Homer pay-parking sign and Seymour rush-hour no-stopping schedule. Other views show meters, restriction symbols or changed infrastructure; several remain inconclusive.

Recommended app treatment: remove these unverified downtown estimates from the Free results, retaining their original ticket evidence and this audit. Restore only individually located curb sections after establishing their complete sign rules and limits. Do not turn a single inspected sign into a precise whole-block rule.

Following user review, these 14 estimates were removed from the free dataset and added to the build exclusions. Further sign-by-sign traversal is needed to identify any actual free curb sections within these blocks.
