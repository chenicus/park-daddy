# Sunset Beach concession: unmarked curb audit (2026-09-27)

Scope: public car-parking gaps within walking distance of the concession at 1204 Beach Avenue. Existing Park Daddy markings were treated as already correct. These observations do not establish a new free curb; no parking record was added from parked vehicles alone.
