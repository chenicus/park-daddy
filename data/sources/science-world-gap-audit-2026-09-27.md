# Science World: sampled unmarked curbs (2026-09-27)

Scope: unmarked public curbs around Science World, with existing Park Daddy markings treated as correct. These are sampled Street View findings, not a complete survey of every street side. No new free, paid, or permit section had both a readable rule and established curb limits.
